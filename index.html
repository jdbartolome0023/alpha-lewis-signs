What is Google Ads and Why Does it Matter?

A quick plain-English overview so we're all on the same page.
Google Ads puts your business at the top of Google's search results when someone types in a keyword related to your products. For example, when a cafe owner in Melbourne searches "a frame sign for cafe", your ad can appear at the very top of the page — above all other businesses — before they even see organic (non-paid) results.

You only pay when someone clicks on your ad. There is no charge for impressions (people seeing it). This is why it's called Pay-Per-Click (PPC) advertising.

The ad spend (the amount that goes to Google) is completely separate from the management fee. You have full visibility and control over your budget at all times — it's charged directly to you by Google.

What Happens Next

Once you confirm the budget, here is what the setup process looks like.

  • Budget Confirmation

    You confirm your preferred monthly ad spend from the options above. This is the amount that will go directly to Google.

  • Google Ads Account Setup

    We set up your Google Ads account, connect it to your website, and install conversion tracking so we can see exactly which clicks are turning into enquiries or sales.

  • Campaign Build

    We build your campaigns using the keywords from this research, write the ad copy, and set up targeting for all of Australia. Before anything goes live, you will have the opportunity to review and approve.

  • Go Live

    Your ads go live. Within the first 48 hours you will start appearing at the top of Google for your target keywords.

  • Weekly Reporting

    You receive a weekly summary of how your ads are performing — clicks, spend, and any leads generated. After 4 to 6 weeks, we will have enough data to make informed decisions about what to scale and what to adjust.
