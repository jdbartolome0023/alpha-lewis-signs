## Questions to ask, in order

1. **Ad rank.** We lose 72% to 76% of Search impressions to ad rank, even though ad relevance is above average and expected click-through rate is average. Landing page experience is below average. What exactly on the pages is Google measuring, and what moves that rating fastest for an online sign shop?

2. **Bidding.** We are on Maximize clicks with a A$5 cap because there are no conversions yet. Is that the right setting at this stage, and how many conversions do we need before moving to Maximize conversions?

3. **Budget.** At A$16 a day we get about 7 Search clicks a day. Is that enough for the account to learn, or would a smaller number of ad groups with the same budget work better?

4. **Keywords and match types.** Many keywords are phrase match head terms such as "sign", "a frame sign" and "custom sign". Which match types and keyword themes do you recommend for a business selling signs at A$159 to A$548?

5. **Mobile.** Mobile is 86% of Search clicks with no sales. Is a mobile bid adjustment sensible, and does Google show any mobile specific issue for the site?

6. **Shopping.** The Walter A-frame (click-through rate 0.8%) and Mabel (0.8%) are the weakest products. Does Merchant Center have a price competitiveness report for our products, and what does it show?

7. **Automatically created sitelinks.** Google created 13 sitelinks in the account that I did not set up (several pointed to the homepage, one said "Select Custom Sizes, Shapes"). I removed them on 10 November. Where is the setting that stops Google creating them again? It is not under Account settings or the campaign settings in our screen.

8. **Tracking.** Purchases and the funnel events reach Google Analytics through two Shopify custom pixels, because the checkout does not load the site's Tag Manager. How can we check that a purchase from an ad click will be credited to the ad in Google Ads?

9. **Secondary conversions.** I am importing add_to_cart and begin_checkout as secondary conversions so we can measure progress before the first sale. Is there anything to avoid when doing that?

10. **Benchmarks.** What conversion rate and cost per click are normal for online signage in Australia, so I can judge whether A$2.35 per click is reasonable?
