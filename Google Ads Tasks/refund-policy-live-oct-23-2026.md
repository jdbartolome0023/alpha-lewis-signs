# Signee refund policy: live text (copied from https://signeesigns.com.au/policies/refund-policy on Oct 23 2026)

Approved by Nic and Kim on WhatsApp (Oct 21 to 23 2026) and posted by Josette in Shopify Admin > Settings > Policies > Refund policy. This copy was read from the live page, not from the WhatsApp message.

## Damaged or faulty goods
If your order arrives damaged or with a manufacturing fault, contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@signeesigns.com.au with your order number and clear photos of the damage. In line with Australian Consumer Law, you are entitled to a remedy (replacement, repair or refund) for goods that arrive faulty or not as described.

## Change of mind
We do not accept returns for change of mind on blank or branded orders. All Signee® products are made to order and we are unable to resell returned goods. Please review your order carefully before placing it. If you have questions about a product before ordering, contact us at hello@signeesigns.com.au.

## Branded orders
Orders that include vinyl-cut graphics are produced to your specification and cannot be returned unless the product arrives faulty or the graphics have been applied incorrectly. Please ensure your artwork is correct before submitting.

## Installation damage
Signee® is not liable for damage caused during or after installation, including damage from improper wall fixing, unsuitable mounting surfaces, or physical impact after delivery.

For full shipping and returns information, see our Shipping & Returns page: https://signeesigns.com.au/policies/shipping-policy
