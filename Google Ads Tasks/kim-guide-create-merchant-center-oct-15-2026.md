**Step 1: Sign in to Google**
1. Open https://merchants.google.com/ in Google Chrome.
2. If Google asks you to sign in, use your Gmail account, the same Google login you use for your Signee Google Ads account.

1. Click **Get started** (it may say **Sign up** or **Create account**).
2. Business name: type `Signee®`
3. Country: **Australia**
4. Click **Continue**.

If Google asks whether you manage accounts for other businesses or are an agency, choose **No**. You only need a standard account for Signee.

Read and accept the terms of service when prompted. Google may also ask a few questions about what you sell. Choose the closest answers (online store, signs). Nothing here affects your billing, because Merchant Center is free.
