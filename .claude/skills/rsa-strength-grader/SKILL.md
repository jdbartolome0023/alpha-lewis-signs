# rsa-strength-grader

*Cluster: Ad Copy & Assets . Part of the 35 Google Ads Skills set by Vicky Lalwani, Digital Marketing Director.*

## Purpose

Diagnose why an RSA's ad strength is below Excellent and give the specific asset changes - more headlines, more variety, keyword inclusion, less pinning - to lift it.

## When to use

- Ad strength is Poor or Average
- The user wants to maximize ad strength
- Reviewing RSAs before a campaign launch
- Diagnosing weak ad performance tied to ad strength

## Inputs required

- The current RSA assets: headlines, descriptions, pinning (required)
- The target keyword and ad group theme (required)
- Optional: current ad strength rating, performance data

## Step-by-step instructions

1. Review the current assets: how many headlines and descriptions, the variety of angles, keyword inclusion, and pinning.
2. Diagnose what's dragging strength: too few headlines (aim for 15), low variety (repeated angles), missing keyword, over-pinning (locks combinations), or weak descriptions.
3. For each gap, give the specific fix: add headlines in missing angles, include the keyword in more headlines, unpin to free up combinations, strengthen descriptions.
4. Provide the actual replacement or additional assets, within character limits.
5. Note that ad strength is a guide, not a ranking factor itself - but higher strength generally correlates with better performance.
6. Output the diagnosis, the specific asset changes, and the expected strength lift.

## Output format

An ad-strength diagnosis: what's dragging it down, the specific assets to add or change (with copy), pinning advice, and the expected lift to Good/Excellent.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Headline count and variety are assessed against best practice (15, varied)
- [ ] Keyword inclusion in headlines is checked
- [ ] Over-pinning is identified as a strength killer
- [ ] Specific replacement/additional assets are provided
- [ ] The 'ad strength is a guide, not a ranking factor' caveat is noted

## Optimization notes

Ad strength measures asset quantity and variety, not guaranteed performance - but the two usually move together because variety lets Google find winners. The most common strength killer is over-pinning: locking headlines to positions stops the machine from testing combinations. Pin only what must always show; let the rest compete.
