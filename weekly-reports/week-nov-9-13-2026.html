  • Spending is steady at about A$32 a day, which is the new daily budget of A$16 for the search ads and A$16 for Google Shopping. A click on a search ad cost about A$2.10 and a click on a Shopping ad about A$1.43.

November 9–11

What I did

  • Done

    Looked closely at why visitors are not buying yet. Google rates the experience on the pages visitors land on as below average for most searches, and on phones people stay about 87 seconds compared with 181 seconds on a computer. About 1 in 5 visitors from the ads reaches a product page, and about 1 in 20 of those add a sign to the cart. That is where the biggest gain is.

  • Done

    Prepared three small website changes in a private copy of the theme, so nothing has changed on the live site: the price shown under each sign on the collection pages, the signs placed above the long introduction text, and a short dispatch line under the Add to cart button. I sent Nic the preview links, and he gave the go ahead. He also asked for the wording “if you add artwork”, which I updated. Thank you Nic.

  • Done

    Removed 13 extra links that Google had added to the ads on its own. Several sent people to the home page instead of a sign, and none of them were set up by me. I will keep checking that Google does not add them again.

  • Done

    Paused 7 search terms that were costing money without leading anywhere (mostly “light box” and “backlit sign” searches). Together they used A$129 and 56 clicks, with no sales. They can be switched back on at any time.

  • Done

    Checked Mini Flossy order #1010 (A$228) from Monday. It looks like a direct visit rather than an ad click, and the purchase tracking recorded it correctly, including the Shop Pay checkout.

  • Done

    Used the 50% margin from Kim and Nic to work out what each click can sensibly cost. At that margin, about 1.6% to 2.4% of search ad visitors need to buy for the ads to break even. Thank you both for answering so quickly.

  • Done

    Kept the reporting connections to the ad account running, and checked the Google Tag Manager setup. Nothing needed to change there.

  • Started

    Setting up tracking for “add to cart” and “begin checkout” as extra progress signals. Until the first sale lands, these show whether the page changes are helping. They will not affect how the ads are bought.
